Delete account and data

How to delete your Estimaster online account and all associated data – in the app or by e-mail.

In the app (immediately)

1. Open Estimaster.

2. Profile → gear icon (Settings) → section “Online”.

3. Tap “Delete online account” and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately and permanently. Your progress on the phone remains until you remove it under Settings → “Delete all data” or uninstall the app.

Without the app (by e-mail)

Write to planer.apps.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Estimaster account” and state your player name and your friend code (shown in the app under Friends). If you have linked your account to Google, please write from that Google address. We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm the deletion.

What is deleted

  • the account ID and, if linked, the Google link (Google ID, e-mail address, name),
  • player name and friend code,
  • your daily results and friendships,
  • duels you started; in other players’ duels your name is removed,
  • question reports you sent with your account.

Nothing is retained. Anonymous estimates for the player average are not attributed to any person and remain as a summary per question.
