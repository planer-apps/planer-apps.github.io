Privacy policy

This policy explains which personal data the app “Estimaster” (German: “Schätzmeister”) and our website process, for what purpose, on which legal basis, and what rights you have. As of 21 November 2026. This is a translation; in case of doubt the German version applies.

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Tom Wagler, Bontjes-van-Beek-Straße 25, 04349 Leipzig, Germany, e-mail: planer.apps.dev@gmail.com

We are not required to appoint a data protection officer. If you have questions about data protection, please write to the e-mail address above.

2. The essentials

  • Estimaster can be played fully without an account and without internet. Your progress is stored on your phone.
  • An online account for leaderboards, friends and duels is optional, only available from age 16 and only needs a player name – no e-mail address.
  • For the “player average” we transmit your estimates without a name and without an identifier.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or tracking services and no crash reports to third parties. We do not sell data.

3. Data on your device

Your progress is only stored on your smartphone: an optional name, your estimates and points, daily challenge results, streaks, records, achievements, reported questions (until they are transmitted) and settings. You can delete everything under Settings → “Delete all data”; uninstalling the app also deletes the data.

The app does not take part in the automatic Android backup to the Google cloud. Only if you move directly to a new phone (e.g. by cable or Samsung Smart Switch) does Android transfer the app data from device to device. We have no access to this.

4. Synchronisation with the server – even without an account

Once a day the app downloads new questions and the daily questions from the server. No data about you is transmitted in the process.

  • Player average: after each estimate we transmit the question ID, the estimated number and the points achieved – without a name, account ID or device ID. Only a summary per question is stored (count, average, distribution of points), from which nobody can draw conclusions about individuals.
  • Reporting a question: if you report a question as incorrect, we transmit the question ID, the selected reason, your optional note and the app version. For this the app creates a random, anonymous identifier in the background so that the server can limit abuse.

Purpose and legal basis: a working game with the same daily questions for everyone, comparison with other players and correct questions (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete reports once they have been processed, at the latest after 12 months.

5. Online account (optional, from age 16)

You can create an online account for leaderboards, the weekly league, friends and duels. We then process:

  • a random account ID (anonymous sign-in, without e-mail and without password),
  • your self-chosen player name (2 to 20 characters) and a random friend code,
  • your daily challenge results (points, your estimates and the questions played) and the app version,
  • friendships (who added whom via friend code or a duel) and duels (questions, estimates, points of both sides),
  • technical data with every connection: IP address, time and type of request. Our service provider processes these to operate the service securely and to prevent abuse.

Who sees what: in leaderboards all signed-in players only see player name, points and rank. Your friends also see your daily points; in a duel both sides see each other’s estimates. Your friend code is only seen by people you give it to. Please do not use your full name as player name.

Purpose and legal basis: provision of the online features under the terms of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); the technical data is based on our legitimate interest in operating the service securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Storage period: until you delete the account (Settings → “Delete online account”). Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). On the server, each account’s data is separated from all others by access rules.

6. Sign in with Google (optional)

You can link your online account to Google (“Sign in with Google” or “Secure account with Google”) to restore it on another phone. You then sign in with Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google then sends us your Google ID, your e-mail address, your name and, if applicable, the link to your profile picture. We only use this information for signing in; leaderboards continue to show only your player name. Google’s privacy policy applies to signing in with Google. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. Service providers and transfers to third countries

We use Supabase as processor (Art. 28 GDPR) for the server and the online account. Provider: Supabase Pte. Ltd., 65 Chulia Street #38-02/03, OCBC Centre, Singapore 049513. The data is stored encrypted in a data centre in Ireland (EU, region eu-west-1). The data processing agreement (Data Processing Addendum) is part of Supabase’s terms.

Supabase is based in Singapore and uses further service providers (list at supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/subprocessor-list), some of them in the USA. Access from outside the EU can therefore not be ruled out. Such transfers are safeguarded by the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR), which are part of the data processing agreement.

Questions and photos: many questions are based on Wikidata (public domain, CC0); the photos come from Wikimedia Commons (author and licence are shown with each question). The photos are included in the app. Only for questions that are later downloaded from the server may a photo be loaded directly from Wikimedia (Wikimedia Foundation, Inc., San Francisco, USA); Wikimedia then technically receives your IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Google Play: the app is distributed via Google Play. Google itself is responsible for downloads, updates and your Google account; we only receive aggregated statistics from Google that cannot be linked to you.

8. Other features on the device

  • Reminders: the daily reminder for the daily challenge is triggered by Android on your device. For this the app asks for the “Notifications” permission. There are no push messages from our server.
  • Sharing: the result image and duel codes are created on the device. You decide what you pass on via “Share”.
  • Sound and vibration are only output on the device.

9. This website

The website is provided via GitHub Pages: GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data (IP address, time, page requested, browser) to deliver and secure the site. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. How long GitHub keeps these logs is set out in GitHub’s privacy statement. Data may be transferred to the USA. According to GitHub, such transfers are based on EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR) and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

Contact form: if you write to us using the form on the website, we store your message, the e-mail address provided and the time with Supabase (see section 7) in order to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. We delete the message once the request has been dealt with, at the latest after 12 months.

The website does not use cookies or analytics services and does not load content from external servers (no external fonts or scripts).

10. Children and young people

Estimaster is aimed at young people aged 16 and over and adults. Without an account, no data that can be attributed to a person is transmitted. An online account can only be created from age 16; you confirm your age when doing so. If someone under 16 has nevertheless created an account, we delete it as soon as we become aware of it. Parents can contact us for this.

11. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access to your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) – you can change your player name directly in the app,
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) – in the app under Settings → Online → “Delete online account” or at planer-apps.github.io/schaetzmeister/en/konto-loeschen.html,
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR).

Please contact planer.apps.dev@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). We cannot attribute anonymous estimates for the player average to any person and therefore cannot hand them out or delete them individually.

12. No automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions and do not create profiles about you. Points, leaderboards and leagues result solely from your estimates in the game.

13. Changes

We adapt this privacy policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The current version is always available in the app and at planer-apps.github.io/schaetzmeister/en/datenschutz.html.
