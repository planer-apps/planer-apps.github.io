How scoring works

What counts is the ratio between your estimate and the correct value. If you're 10% off, you get 800 points – and under 1% deviation it's a bullseye.

  • Twice as much or half as much gives the same points.
  • Big and small numbers are equally fair.
  • Every value has a source – you can report wrong questions.

Play without data collection

Estimaster works without an account and without internet. Playing online is optional.

  • Without an account: your progress stays on your phone.
  • Online account (16+): just a player name – no email, no password. Servers in the EU.
  • No ads, no trackers, no selling of data.
  • Delete your account in the app at any time – or find out how here.

FAQ

How much does Estimaster cost?

Nothing. The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where do the numbers come from?

From public sources such as Wikidata. Every question shows its source and date, photos show author and license. If you find a mistake, report the question right in the app.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. You can play completely offline. For leaderboards, friends and duels, players aged 16+ create an online account – with just a player name.

Which languages are available?

12 languages. The app picks your phone's language automatically, and you can change it at any time in the settings. Players of all languages share the same leaderboards and duels.

Is there an iPhone version?

The Android version comes first.

How can I contact you?

Via the contact form or by email to planer.apps.dev@gmail.com.
